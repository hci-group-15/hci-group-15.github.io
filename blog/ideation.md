Reflection on the brainstorming method
For the brainstorming method we wrote our ideas on colorful paper squares in an anonymized round-robin fashion, which made the process very engaging for everyone and allowed all members to contribute a large quantity of ideas, without fear of judgement for "bad ideas". We found this method worked very well in terms of idea generation, and also was great fun for all participants. In order to get inspired before each of the 6 ideas-generation rounds, we read a couple of user / "how might we" needs out loud, but also emphasized that these prompts only served for voluntary inspiration; any idea not related to the prompt could be submitted. We introduced this strategy because some members felt overwhelmed with the prospect of generating 6 ideas "out of thin air". Judging by the team atmosphere during this process and by the impressive results, we felt that this approach worked very well. 

Additionally, after all generated ideas were presented to the group in an anonymized manner (the person reading out load was not able to match handwriting to individuals of the group), some group members felt further inspired by the proposed ideas and contributed even more ideas in an unofficial 7th round. This indicates that some ideas were easy to build upon, so in future it would also be interesting to use the other brainstorming method which builds on previous ideas.
